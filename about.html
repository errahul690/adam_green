About us

The software layer for the energy transition

Energy infrastructure is becoming increasingly connected. Solar farms, wind turbines, batteries, EV chargers, and smart meters are producing more data than ever before.

But data by itself isn’t enough. Businesses need technology that can transform that data into visibility, intelligence, and actionable insights.

That’s what Adam Greens is building.

Our mission

Make renewable-energy infrastructure smarter through software.

We are developing technology that helps businesses understand and manage their energy assets through connected, cloud-based intelligence.

Connect energy assets. Understand their data. Turn insights into action.

Our vision

A world where every energy asset can be connected, understood, and optimized.

The future of energy will be increasingly renewable, connected, and data-driven. Adam Greens aims to provide the software intelligence that helps businesses operate within that future.

Why Adam Greens?
