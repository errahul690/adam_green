Platform & solutions

One intelligent platform for renewable energy

Renewable-energy assets generate valuable operational data every day. Adam Greens helps businesses bring this information together through a cloud-based software platform designed to provide greater visibility, analytics, and intelligence across their energy infrastructure.

Capabilities

What our platform can do

Asset Monitoring

Get a centralized view of connected energy assets and monitor their operational status and performance.

Performance Analytics

Compare actual performance with expected performance to identify inefficiencies and unusual patterns.

Predictive Insights

Use data patterns to identify potential issues and support proactive maintenance and operational planning.

Real-Time Alerts

Receive alerts when important performance or operational conditions require attention.

Energy Forecasting

Use historical and real-time data to better understand and forecast renewable-energy generation.

Environmental Reporting

Analyze renewable-energy performance and support data-driven environmental reporting.
